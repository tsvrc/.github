# Governance

TsVRC is currently maintained by one person, Antony Seas ([@ToniSeas](https://github.com/ToniSeas)),
across every repo in this org. This document describes how decisions get made today, and
how that can change as the project grows.

## Decision-making

The maintainer has final say on what gets merged, released, and how the project is
directed, informed by discussion in issues, pull requests, and
[Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/tsvrc/discussions). There's no formal RFC process
or voting body right now, that's the right amount of process for a project at this size,
not a permanent design choice.

## Becoming a co-maintainer

There's no fixed bar or waiting period. In practice, someone becomes a trusted
contributor by submitting good PRs consistently, engaging constructively on issues and
reviews, and demonstrating they understand the project's design decisions well enough to
uphold them. The maintainer grants triage or merge rights when that trust is there,
starting narrow (triage, or merge rights on a specific area) rather than all at once.

## When this changes

If TsVRC grows to have multiple active maintainers, expect this document to be rewritten
to reflect real shared decision-making (a steering group, defined merge rights per area,
etc.) rather than staying a placeholder for a structure that no longer matches reality.
